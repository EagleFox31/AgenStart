## Application logos and identification artwork

AgenStart bundles local application artwork solely to identify software in the recommendation, review, installation, history, reporting, and public product-preview experiences. The artwork is packaged locally so desktop screens remain usable offline and the public site can reuse the same canonical assets at build time.

Product names, logos, trademarks, and registered trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. A source collection's software/content license does **not** waive or transfer trademark rights in the brands represented by its assets.

AgenStart's use of application artwork:

- is for nominative product identification only;
- does not imply sponsorship, partnership, certification, endorsement, or affiliation with AgenStudio or AgenStart;
- does not transfer any trademark or brand rights to AgenStudio;
- must not be used to suggest that AgenStart is an official distribution channel for the represented product;
- should follow the relevant publisher's current brand/trademark guidance where applicable;
- may be removed or replaced when a publisher requests it or when applicable brand guidance changes.

AgenStart installs software only through the trusted provider metadata defined in its catalogue. Displayed artwork does not change package trust, compatibility, licensing, or installation-source decisions.
